# Product Identity Quality Gates

Approve only when applicable checks have direct visual or build evidence.

## Mark

- One dominant reading; no damaging accidental symbol or close adjacent-brand resemblance.
- Works in one color, grayscale, light, dark, and reverse without explanatory copy.
- Curves, joins, negative space, and optical alignment remain intentional.
- Typography is licensed; repeated inline SVGs use unique IDs.

## Size and contexts

Inspect native renders at 16, 20, 24, 32, and 64 px. Cuts and counters must stay open, padding balanced, and the silhouette recognizable. Inspect the real favicon, header, onboarding, app tile, installer, and social preview surfaces the product uses.

## Assets and release

- Canonical editable SVG plus monochrome/reverse forms.
- App-icon master and platform-generated formats where applicable.
- Palette, typeface, safe area, source, and export provenance recorded.
- Stale assets, imports, and metadata removed; build, typecheck, and packaging checks pass.
- Source, built, deployed, and fetched hashes compared before adding favicon cache-busting.

State the active direction, preserved alternates, verified surfaces, known issues, and whether trademark clearance remains outstanding.
